## Prompt 1

We are going to turn everything that must be done in this block into numbered pending items, each one carrying its full specification. Read `PROJECT.md`, `docs/SEMANTIC_TREE.md`, the client's request for this block, and whatever is in `docs/STATUS.md`. If the request is ambiguous on any point such that you cannot write a verifiable pending item for it, ask me; do not resolve it by assuming. Then create or extend `docs/PENDING.md` with one item per thing to do, each with: number (PND-001 onwards); one-line title; the node of the semantic tree it hangs from; the user story or use case that originates it, in the client's words; the detailed description; the business rules that apply, each with its decision and date, or marked as "rule missing, asked"; the data in and out; the screens affected; the edge cases and exceptions; the acceptance criteria in the form "given, when, then"; how it will be verified as done; and status. If a node the item needs does not exist in the tree, propose it and mark it. Order the items by the priority I gave you; if I gave none, propose an order and tell me why. This block also carries the hooks of Annex H. Every pending item that touches a screen declares, in `docs/PENDING.md`: the group of convergence it belongs to (Annex H, B1), the profiles that have it, and one acceptance criterion per affected profile, written as "given profile X, this person can do Y and cannot do Z". It also adds or updates its row in `docs/PROFILE_MATRIX.md`: the functionality, its group, its profiles and its entry route. A pending item that touches a screen without that row is not planned: the row is written here, not invented later in the code. The catalogue lives in `docs/ANEXO_HEURISTICAS.md`: cite it, do not restate it. When you need a decision from me, ask it with the four parts of UX-28 (`docs/ANEXO_HEURISTICAS.md`) and in this order: the data that makes the question necessary, listed; one paragraph of at most five lines that links that data and lets the question be seen; the question itself with its possible answers numbered; and your recommendation. Say it at the technical depth the project declared in `communication_depth` -- `beginner`, `decision_maker` or `technical`; if the project declared none, use `decision_maker`. Reply in my language.
