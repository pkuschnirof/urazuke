## Prompt 1

Build `docs/PRODUCER_CONSUMER_MAP.md`: for every contract in the system (a service, a table, a file format, a list of values), who produces it and who consumes it. If there are parts of the system you cannot access or do not understand, tell me instead of leaving them out of the map. And the map carries one more family, which is the ports you doubled for testing: `ports_with_a_test_double`, with `ports_total`, how many ports of the system have a double, and `ports_with_a_crossing_run`, how many of those have at least one run that crosses them to the real other side; and then, per port, its adapter, its double, and `crossing_run`: the exact command, and what it reached on the other side, which is `browser`, `operating_system`, `database`, `network` or `none`. The other side is whatever the port is doubling, and another double is never the other side (Annex H, CQ-21.1). `none` is a declared hole and it is worth more than silence: a port whose double no run crosses is where four dead controls fit underneath a green suite. Do not write a crossing run you did not run, and count a port with no crossing run inside `ports_total` instead of leaving it out so the figure reads better.
