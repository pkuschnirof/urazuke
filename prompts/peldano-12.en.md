## Prompt 1

When you report, separate three blocks: VERIFIED (what you tested, with the command and its output), BELIEVED (what you think works but did not test, and why not), and BLIND SPOTS (what you cannot see from where you are: another environment, real data, the user's browser). Cover every item I asked for, one by one, even when the answer is "I did not do it". No claim without its proof next to it. Do not say "done": say what is done and how you know it. If something went wrong, say it first. When you report, also report the Annex H groups: what was measured, what only warns and why, and which rule is switched off with its reason, who authorised it and its review date. Report the two dashboard figures with the fingerprint they were computed with (catalogue version, active and switched-off rules, frontend adapter, tool versions) and with the account of any movement. No figure closes a block: the green is decided rule by rule, and a switched-off rule with no written exception turns the whole report red. When you need a decision from me, ask it with the four parts of UX-28 (`docs/ANEXO_HEURISTICAS.md`) and in this order: the data that makes the question necessary, listed; one paragraph of at most five lines that links that data and lets the question be seen; the question itself with its possible answers numbered; and your recommendation. Say it at the technical depth the project declared in `communication_depth` -- `beginner`, `decision_maker` or `technical`; if the project declared none, use `decision_maker`. Reply in my language.
