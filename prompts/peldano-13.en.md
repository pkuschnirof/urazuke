## Prompt 1

Audit this system for [scope]. Read-only: change nothing except writing your report to `docs/AUDIT_[date].md`. If auditing some part requires access you lack (the database, the log, an environment), tell me and mark that part as not audited; do not pass it as fine. Go through all of the code, not a sample. Pick two or three pending items closed in the last cycle and reproduce their receipts literally: rerun the exact red-then-green commands they cite against the commits they name, and say for each one whether it reproduces or not. A receipt you only read is not a receipt you audited. For every finding: where it is (file and line), what the risk is, what evidence proves it (the command, the query, the number), and its priority: P0 must be fixed now, P1 soon, P2 when possible. Give a grade to every dimension you audited. End with the list of P0 findings turned into proposed pending items. This report carries declarations that a gate reads, each one under its fixed key, so write the key in English even when the report is in my language. `auditor_choice`: which case applies and who decided it -- `different_model` if you are a model other than the one that built this system; `same_model_declared` if you are the same model because no other one was up to the task; `tool_constraint` if what chose you was a key that happened to be loaded, a timeout or any other limit of the tool, and then say so with those words. In the `Auditor:` line, the model that built and the model that audits. `evidence_register_health`: the health of the register of the evidence, which is a dimension of this audit and is not optional -- how many functional commits carry no receipt of any kind; how many pending items closed as shipped lack their receipts, and of those how many have the reason written; and of the receipts that carry a mutation, how many can be applied again. Each of those figures measured by a program, with the command next to it, and a figure you could not measure declared as not measured and never as zero. If the register of the evidence is broken, every other finding in this report rests on data nobody can reproduce. `findings_by_destination`: every finding assigned to one destination -- `product` if the defect is in the audited system, `method` if it is in the method being followed, `tool` if it is in the tool that runs it -- with the register where its pending item goes. A finding whose destination nobody assigned leaves this audit open. Four more declarations, about this report and not about the system, because a gate can read the trace of the work and cannot read the work. `audit_completeness`: `complete` when you wrote down every finding you found, and next to it `findings_announced` and `findings_written` with their figures. Those two figures have to be the same figure: if you announce nine findings and write four, this report is not complete and this step does not close with it -- so do not announce a count you are not going to write, and do not stop halfway and emit your closing marker as if you had finished. `dimension_grades`: the grade of every dimension you audited, each one with the name of its dimension next to it. `receipts_reproduced`: for every receipt you reran, `reproduces` or `does_not_reproduce`, with the command and its output; at least one, and a receipt you only read is not one of them. `not_audited`: `none` when you audited everything in the scope, or `with_pending` and then, part by part, what you did not audit, why, and the pending item that is open for it. A part named as not audited and left without its own pending item leaves this report open; an audit with a smaller scope declared up front is worth more than a wide one with holes. When you need a decision from me, ask it with the four parts of UX-28 (`docs/ANEXO_HEURISTICAS.md`) and in this order: the data that makes the question necessary, listed; one paragraph of at most five lines that links that data and lets the question be seen; the question itself with its possible answers numbered; and your recommendation. Say it at the technical depth the project declared in `communication_depth` -- `beginner`, `decision_maker` or `technical`; if the project declared none, use `decision_maker`. Reply in my language.
