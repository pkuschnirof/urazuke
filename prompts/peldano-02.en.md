We are going to define the project in writing. Read the business survey in `docs/INITIAL_SURVEY.md` and the working agreement in `docs/WORKING_AGREEMENT.md`. Evaluate whether the survey is sufficient to describe the system, its users, its domain and its boundaries; if it is not, ask me for what is missing before writing anything. Then write two things. Second, `docs/SEMANTIC_TREE.md`: the system as a tree, with the domains at the top level and, under each one, its actors, its processes (the use cases, each with actor, trigger, steps, outcome and exceptions), its entities with their attributes, states and transitions, its screens, and its external integrations. Mark every node you inferred rather than read in the survey, so that I can confirm or correct it. Third, the two prior inputs that Annex H needs before a single screen exists: the user glossary, with the words people actually use for each thing as the business says them and not as the system calls them; and the list of profiles, saying who each one is and what they come to do. Take both from the survey, ask me for whatever is not in it instead of inventing it, write them as `docs/GLOSSARY.md` and `docs/PROFILES.md`, name them in `PROJECT.md`, and mark what you inferred there too. Separately, create `docs/STATUS.md` for the living state (what is being worked on, what is open): that file changes every day; the other two change only when the system changes. When you need a decision from me, ask it with the four parts of UX-28 (`docs/ANEXO_HEURISTICAS.md`) and in this order: the data that makes the question necessary, listed; one paragraph of at most five lines that links that data and lets the question be seen; the question itself with its possible answers numbered; and your recommendation. Say it at the technical depth the project declared in `communication_depth` -- `beginner`, `decision_maker` or `technical`; if the project declared none, use `decision_maker`. Reply in my language.
